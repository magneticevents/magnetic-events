Privacy policy.

How we handle personal data when you visit our website or contact us about an event.

1. Data controller and contact

The controller is Tomáš Wolf, Czech company ID (IČO) 08817740, Krásná 239, 739 04 Krásná, Czech Republic, operating as Magnetic Events. For data protection questions, contact poptavky@magneticevents.cz.

2. What we process and why

Enquiry forms may contain your name, email address, event date and location, guest count, event type, requested equipment and message. We use this information to respond, prepare offers and discuss potential orders. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), or, for business contacts, our legitimate interest in business communications (Article 6(1)(f)).

The quotation form requires service type, name/company, email and description. Date, venue and guest count are optional. The contact form requires name, email and message. Without the required data, we may not be able to respond. Please do not include sensitive personal information or unnecessary information about guests.

When a contract is agreed, we process data necessary to perform it and meet legal obligations, including accounting (Article 6(1)(b) and (c)). Service providers may process technical records such as IP addresses to operate and protect their systems. Fraud prevention and defence of legal claims are based on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

3. Recipients and service providers

Formspree, Inc. processes submitted forms and delivers them to poptavky@magneticevents.cz. We use Google Workspace for email, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Google Fonts for fonts. These providers may receive technical request data. People helping us handle enquiries and operate our services may have access, as may authorities when required by law. Provider information: Formspree, Google and GitHub.

4. Transfers outside the EU

These providers may process personal data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. Such transfers must comply with Chapter V GDPR, such as an adequacy decision for the relevant recipient or standard contractual clauses. Contact the controller for information about applicable safeguards.

5. Retention period

We keep enquiry data while handling the request and any related negotiations. Afterwards, we retain only what is needed for a contract, statutory record-keeping, or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. The duration depends on the matter and applicable legal deadlines. Data no longer needed for these purposes is removed. Technical logs and form service archives are also governed by the providers' settings and policies.

6. Your rights

Subject to GDPR requirements, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Handling an enquiry does not require marketing consent. Send your request to poptavky@magneticevents.cz. You can complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection. We do not use automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects or profiling.

7. Analytics, advertising measurement and consent

With your consent we use Google Analytics 4 to measure website traffic and successful form submissions. No names, emails or message content are included. The legal basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which may be changed or withdrawn in cookie settings. Without consent, the analytics script is not started.

With separate advertising measurement consent, Analytics also shares form conversion events and ad click identifiers with the linked Google Ads account to attribute enquiries, evaluate cost and optimise ads. Names, emails and message content are excluded. Personalised ads and remarketing remain disabled. Google Ireland Limited provides the measurement services; international data transfer information is explained above.
