Marquee hire.
Room for your event.

Marquees for municipal festivals, company events, conferences and concerts. We recommend the right size for your guest count, programme and venue. Installation, anchoring and dismantling of large marquees are provided by our team.

01 / Marquee hire

What we can supply

Modular event halls

10 m wide structures, extendable in 5 m sections. Available layouts: 10 × 5, 10 × 10, 10 × 15, 10 × 20, 10 × 25, 10 × 30, 10 × 35, 10 × 40 and 10 × 50 m (50–500 m²). Height approx. 4.8 m; eaves 2.5 m. White flame-retardant covers. Installation and anchoring included.

Other marquee sizes

Additional structures: 12 × 5, 12 × 10, 12 × 15, 20 × 6 and 6 × 5 m. Construction type, height and availability are confirmed for your venue and date.

Pagodas

Freestanding 4 × 4 m and 5 × 5 m pagodas for check-in, catering, displays and sheltered seating. Flooring is available on request.

Pop-up gazebos

Folding marquees in sizes 2 × 2, 3 × 3, 6 × 3, 7.5 × 3, 9 × 3 and 8 × 4 m. Suitable for stalls, refreshments and registration.

Star tents & TeePee

A distinctive 8 × 12 m star-shaped tent ('Chobotnice') with an approximate 96 m² footprint; TeePee options on request. The anchoring method and suitability of the ground must be checked.

Fabric canopies

Decorative fabric shelter elements planned to suit the specific space and mounting points.

03 / FAQ

Common questions

How is the hire price calculated?

It depends on size, location, hire period, transport, installation and extra equipment. We prepare an individual quotation.

Can we put up a large marquee ourselves?

No. Large event marquees are assembled, anchored and dismantled by our team.

Can you add flooring and furniture?

Yes. We can quote flooring, beer-table sets, chairs, lighting and other accessories in one request.
